The Challenge & The Solution

Coordinated Care Operations

How ADAPT bridges care coordination gaps to prevent errors and reduce caregiver cognitive load.

cancel Problems in traditional tracking

  • ✕
    Care guidelines get split across paper charts, messaging apps, and separate note tools.
  • ✕
    Patient task progress is hard to audit or cross-reference during caregiver handover windows.
  • ✕
    Missed clinical guidelines or medications are often discovered only after a critical incident occurs.

check_circle The ADAPT Approach

  • ✓
    Create, assign, and customize care routines from a single, centralized web workspace.
  • ✓
    Optimize delivery with a responsive design, rendering patient schedules beautifully on smartphones.
  • ✓
    Feed outcomes directly into unified charts and automatic AI summaries for prompt care decisions.

About ADAPT

Cognitive Assistance for Clinical Care

ADAPT (Advanced Digital Assistant for Patient Tracking) is designed to streamline clinical communication pipelines and safeguard patient wellbeing.

Centralized Management

By housing medical scenario details, guidelines, active tasks, and disease parameters in a single database, ADAPT eliminates data silos, helping caretakers coordinate efficiently.

Cognitive AI Partners

Our integration with Gemini AI functions as a clinical sidekick. It helps scan task logs to identify gaps, highlight critical allergy sensitivities, and generate handover reports.

Designed for Caregivers

With user-centric interface layouts, clear font scaling, and optimized mobile viewport styling, ADAPT keeps caregivers focused on their patients rather than administrative struggles.
